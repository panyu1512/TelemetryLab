# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. This is a hobby project about lap times.

## The standard

Everyone taking part — filing issues, reviewing, contributing, commenting — is
expected to keep it civil and on the substance. That means:

- Criticise the code, the design or the decision, not the person who made it.
- Assume the other person is trying to help, including when they are wrong.
- Take disagreement as far as the argument goes and no further.

Not acceptable, and enough on its own to have a contribution declined or an
account blocked from the repo:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or abuse of any kind.
- Comments about someone's identity — including race, nationality, gender,
  sexuality, disability, age, religion or appearance — used as a way to
  dismiss them.
- Sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Persistently derailing threads after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks the above to the maintainer by opening a
[private security advisory][advisory], which is the one channel here that is
not public, or by contacting [@panyu1512](https://github.com/panyu1512)
directly.

Reports are handled by the maintainer, who will keep the reporter's identity to
themselves. Responses run from a word in a thread through to blocking an
account from the repository, whichever fits.

[advisory]: https://github.com/panyu1512/TelemetryLab/security/advisories/new

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits —
and anywhere someone is representing the project.
